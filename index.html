    02 PROTOCOL

    The grid hides hostile ICE nodes. Reveal every safe node to complete the breach. A number tells you how many hostiles touch that node — diagonals count.

    Your first breach is always safe and opens a pocket of clean data. Flag hostiles to keep track, then chord a number when its surrounding flags match.

    DEEP SCAN reveals a guaranteed safe sector. ICE BREAK instantly flags one hostile. One charge each — spend them wisely.

    SONAR PING sweeps a 5×5 area and reports the hostile count without revealing a single tile. GHOST PROTOCOL is a passive shield that absorbs your first hostile breach. DATA SIPHON banks one token per three correct manual flags; three tokens refund one spent charge, up to twice per run.
